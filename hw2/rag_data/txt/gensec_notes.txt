Generative Security (GenSec) course notes

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retriever over a document
corpus with a generative LLM. Relevant chunks are fetched by embedding
similarity and then passed to the model as context so answers stay grounded
in source material instead of relying only on the model's training data.

Common RAG pipeline steps:
1. Load documents from many formats (HTML, PDF, TXT, JSON, Wikipedia, etc.)
2. Split documents into overlapping chunks
3. Embed chunks into a vector database such as Chroma
4. At query time, embed the question, retrieve top-k chunks, and generate
